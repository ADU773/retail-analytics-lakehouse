Insights

Generated automatically from this run's gold tables (also in docs/insights.md).

  1. Growth
    Net revenue grew 96% year over year

    The last 12 months generated $4.55M of net revenue vs $2.32M in the prior 12 months, on 30,166 orders (102% more). Growth is driven by a compounding customer base: 54% of purchasing customers have ordered more than once.

  2. Seasonality
    Cyber Monday 2025 was the biggest day: 3.9x an average day

    Cyber Monday brought in $48.2K vs a 2025 daily average of $12.5K; the four-day Black Friday-Cyber Monday window delivered $140.0K (3.1% of the year's revenue in 1.1% of its days).

  3. Seasonality
    Sunday is the strongest weekday, 31% above Thursday

    Average daily net revenue ranges from $8,504 on Thursdays to $11.2K on Sundays - schedule e-mail sends and promotions for the weekend peak.

  4. Retention
    16.3% of new customers buy again in their second month

    Averaged over complete cohorts, 16.3% of a cohort is active in month 1, 12.1% in month 6 and 8.6% in month 12. The steepest drop happens right after the first order, so the post-purchase journey is the main retention lever.

  5. Customers
    Champions and Loyal Customers are 29% of buyers but 59% of revenue

    3,531 customers sit in At Risk / Cannot Lose Them with $1.66M of historical revenue - the highest-value win-back audience.

  6. Customers
    Plus membership members purchase 1.9x as often

    Plus members average 3.8 orders each vs 2.0 for Standard customers; Business accounts order less often but at a $364 AOV (3.0x Standard).

  7. Marketing
    Email returns 21.6x its spend; display only 0.87x

    On last-touch attribution, display spent $206.2K for $180.2K of revenue (POAS 0.41 - it loses money after product costs). Customer acquisition cost ranges from $41 (affiliate) to $313 (display). Last-touch under-credits upper-funnel channels, so treat display as a test-and-learn budget.

  8. Funnel
    Mobile drives 56% of sessions but converts at 15.0% vs 28.1% on desktop

    The largest mobile leak is product view -> add to cart (64% of mobile product viewers never add to cart); a faster mobile product page is the highest-leverage CRO test. Note: anonymous traffic is down-sampled in this synthetic dataset, so absolute conversion rates are higher than typical e-commerce - compare devices relative to each other.

  9. Merchandising
    'Solenne Nordic Cleanser' + 'Lumio Everyday Bath Salts' is the strongest bundle (lift 35.3)

    The pair appears in 32 orders; 19% of baskets with the first item also contain the second. 1,491 product pairs clear the minimum-support threshold - candidates for 'frequently bought together' widgets.

  10. Operations
    Apparel has the highest return rate at 8.9%

    The most common reason is 'wrong_size' (overall return rate 4.9% of units); a size guide on Apparel PDPs is the obvious fix.

  11. CLV
    The BG/NBD model forecast 26-week holdout purchases within 9.7% in aggregate

    Fitted on data up to 26 weeks before the as-of date, it predicted 8,051 purchases for 12,055 customers vs 8,916 actual; customer-level MAE 0.78 vs 0.95 for a naive repeat-rate baseline. Average predicted 12-month CLV is $97.01 of gross margin.

  12. Data quality
    7,350 raw rows (0.82%) were quarantined, none silently dropped

    Top reasons: duplicate_record (4,900), orphan_session_id (1,382), non_positive_quantity (374). 392 order timestamps in non-standard formats/time zones were normalised to UTC, 417 orders and 5,044 events arrived in a later batch, and 2,240 customers have SCD2 history.
